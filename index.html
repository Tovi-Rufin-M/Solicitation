Preserving Our Milestones

From the Yearbook Committee • S.Y. 2025–2026

Dear Benefactors, Alumni, Parents, and Friends,

As we stand at the threshold of graduation, we look back at the laughter, relentless late-night studies, and enduring camaraderie that defined our journey. Our graduation yearbook is not merely a collection of photographs; it is a timeless sanctuary of memories, stories, and victories that will live on for generations.

To ensure that every single student can hold a copy of their memories without heavy financial burden, our committee has initiated this sponsorship campaign to subsidize high-quality printing, binding, and photography.

"No student's memories should be left behind due to financial constraint. Your generosity immortalizes our legacy."

Any contribution, large or small, directly lowers the cost per copy for graduating students in need. We humbly appeal to your kind heart to be our patron.
